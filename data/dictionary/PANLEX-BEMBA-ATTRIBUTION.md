# PanLex English-Bemba Dictionary Attribution

Source: PanLex / cointegrated/panlex-meanings
Snapshot represented by the dataset: PanLex 20240301
Source: https://panlex.org/
Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/cointegrated/panlex-meanings
License: CC0 1.0 Universal.

BembaHub uses direct English-Bemba lexical pairs created by joining
the Bemba and English language subsets on PanLex meaning IDs and
retaining single-word expressions.

PanLex asks users to cite:
Kamholz, David, Jonathan Pool, and Susan M. Colowick. 2014.
PanLex: Building a Resource for Panlingual Lexical Translation.
Proceedings of the 9th International Conference on Language Resources
and Evaluation (LREC 2014).
